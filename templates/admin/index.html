Applications & Features

Manage all business app data below. Each application is listed with its models and features.

  • Requisitions – Create and manage requisitions (draft, pending, approved, completed, rejected).
  • Accounts – Tenant accounts, approval, owners; approve companies to grant Company Admin full access to all apps (including Requisitions).
  • Core – Company profiles, bank accounts.
  • Invoices – Invoices and invoice templates.
  • Receipts – Receipts.
  • Quotations – Quotations and templates.
  • Waybills – Waybills, templates, items.
  • Job Orders – Products, orders, job order templates, customers, stores.
  • Expenses – Expenses, categories, attachments.
  • Inventory – Layouts, items, templates, transactions.
  • Clients – Clients.
  • Accounting – Transactions, ledgers, accounts, financial reports.
  • Messaging – Conversations, messages, call sessions.
  • Security & Users – Users, team members (SubUsers), roles (AccountGroup), activity log, sessions.
  • Auth – Django groups and permissions (used by Roles & Permissions).
