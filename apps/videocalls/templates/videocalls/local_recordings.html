My Recordings

Where Are My Recordings?

When you record a video call, the file is automatically saved to your device's Downloads folder. No server storage used!

Desktop (Windows/Mac/Linux)

Open your Downloads folder

Downloads/
Android Phone

Open Files app or Downloads app

Internal Storage > Downloads
iPhone/iPad

Open Files app

Files > On My iPhone > Downloads

File Naming Format

Each recording is saved with a descriptive name:

Example filename:

VideoCall_ABC12345_2024-01-15_10-30-45.webm

Contains:

  • Prefix: VideoCall
  • Room code: ABC12345
  • Date: 2024-01-15
  • Time: 10:30:45

How to Find Your Recordings

1 Open the Downloads folder on your device
2 Look for files starting with VideoCall_
3 Sort by date to find the most recent recording at the top
4 Click to play or drag to another app to share
