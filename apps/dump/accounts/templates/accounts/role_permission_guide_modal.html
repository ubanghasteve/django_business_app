Role & Permission Guide

What are roles and permissions?

A role is a named set of permissions (e.g. "Accountant", "Sales Rep"). When you assign a role to a user, they can do only what that role allows. Permissions are fine-grained actions such as "view expenses", "add invoices", or "delete receipts". Creating clear roles with the right permissions keeps your account secure and makes it easy to onboard new team members.

How to create a role and assign permissions

  1. Give the role a clear name (e.g. "Expense Viewer", "Invoice Manager").
  2. In the Permissions list below the name, check only the actions this role should be allowed to perform.
  3. Use view for read-only access, add to create new records, change to edit, and delete to remove. Grant only what is needed for the job.
  4. Click Save Role. After saving, go to Roles & Permissions and use "Manage members" to assign users to the role.

Best practices

  • Follow the principle of least privilege: grant only the permissions necessary for the role.
  • Use descriptive role names so anyone can understand who should have that role.
  • You can only assign permissions that you yourself have; you cannot grant abilities you don't have.

Example roles and suggested permissions

Below are 17 example roles with typical permission combinations. The exact permissions available in your account may vary by module (expenses, invoices, inventory, etc.). Use these as a starting point and adjust to match your business.

Role name Suggested permissions (what this role can do)
Viewer (read-only) View expenses, view invoices, view receipts, view inventory, view quotations, view job orders. No add/change/delete.
Expense Viewer View expenses and expense categories only.
Expense Entry View and add expenses; view expense categories. Optionally: change/delete own entries only if your app supports it.
Expense Manager View, add, change, and delete expenses; manage expense categories.
Invoice Viewer View invoices and related data only.
Invoice Creator View and add invoices; view customers and products. Optionally change/delete if needed.
Invoice Manager View, add, change, and delete invoices; manage invoice templates and related settings.
Receipt Viewer View receipts only.
Receipt Entry View and add receipts; optionally change/delete.
Inventory Viewer View inventory items, categories, and stock levels only.
Inventory Clerk View and add inventory; adjust stock (if applicable); view categories.
Inventory Manager View, add, change, and delete inventory; manage categories and templates.
Quotation / Sales View and add quotations; view and add invoices; view customers and products.
Job Order Operator View and add job orders; view products and layouts; optionally change status.
Accountant / Finance View and manage expenses, invoices, receipts; view reports. Usually no user/role management.
Company Admin View, add, change, and delete across core modules: expenses, invoices, receipts, inventory, quotations, job orders; manage categories and templates; optionally manage subusers/team members. Does not include creating or editing roles and permissions (reserved for account owner).
Company Profile Viewer View company profile only (name, address, logo, contact details, etc.). No other permissions—no expenses, invoices, inventory, or other modules.

Tip: Start with a small set of permissions and add more only when needed. You can always edit a role later from the Roles list.
