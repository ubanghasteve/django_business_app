How roles work
  • Each role is backed by a Django Group and scoped to your account only.
  • Permissions follow Django’s built-in system (e.g. invoices.add_invoice, expense_management.change_expense).
  • Users assigned to a role automatically inherit its permissions via user.has_perm().
